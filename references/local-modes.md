# Village bus, Nuri Bus, and DRT verification

Local services vary by municipality and operator. A familiar service label does not prove that the same booking, fare, or operating rules apply elsewhere.

## Village bus / 마을버스

Verify the operating authority, route number/name, direction, exact boarding and alighting stops, service days, first/last trip, expected headway, fare, and transfer treatment. Use a map planner for candidate connections, then prefer the municipality or operator for current service facts.

## Nuri Bus and DRT

Verify all of the following from the responsible public authority or operator:

- service area and eligible trip boundaries;
- fixed-stop, virtual-stop, or flexible-route behavior;
- reservation requirement and supported channel;
- operating days/hours and reservation cutoff;
- eligibility or membership restrictions;
- fare and transfer policy;
- accessibility and companion/luggage rules;
- expected pickup window and cancellation rules.

Do not describe DRT as walk-up fixed-route transit unless the operator says so. Do not reserve a ride without a separate explicit request and authorization.

## Comparison

Include access to the pickup point, reservation friction, expected pickup window, transfers, destination-side walking, and a fallback if the service is unavailable. Wider time ranges are appropriate when dispatch timing is uncertain. Label secondary-source-only fields and include the lookup date.
